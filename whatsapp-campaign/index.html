Quiz & game · Rang De Play

Play it here, or open full screen

Jyoti Ben’s journey (lead game). An interactive story: help Jyoti Ben, who makes papad at home, find a loan. The bank, a finance company and the village moneylender each fail her, with a real fact at each dead end. Rang De is revealed only at the end. Jyoti Ben is fictional, and the page says so.

Guess the gap. 4 multiple-choice questions, only about rural credit pain points (NABARD July 2026 and Ministry of Finance Aug 2026 figures, plus rangde.in’s 86%; each answer shows its source). No product questions.

Find your entrepreneur. 3 taps: who, where, how much. Shows 3 real matching entrepreneurs linked to their rangde.in pages, plus “See all live opportunities” and a WhatsApp share.

Swipe to support. Swipe right to shortlist real entrepreneurs, left to skip. Ends with your shortlist and “Share my shortlist on WhatsApp”.

See your ripple. Slide from ₹500 to ₹50,000 and see the ripple: entrepreneurs at ₹500 each, lives you could help impact, and returns up to 8.5% p.a. (rangde.in’s own figures).

Start a friends pool. Pick who to back, how many friends and how much each; it writes the WhatsApp group message with the link.

Every link carries utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=game&utm_campaign=rangde_play, so visits from the game show up separately in analytics.
