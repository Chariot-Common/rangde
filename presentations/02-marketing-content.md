# Rang De · Marketing plan — slide-by-slide content

Marketing deck, 14 slides, 16:9 (1920×1080). It uses the same fonts, colours and layouts as the website case study (see `01-website-redesign-content.md`).

Images are in `screenshots/`. Every figure here comes from rangde.in, NABARD (July 2026), Rang De's Instagram (8 Oct 2026), or the campaign kit itself.

---

### Slide 1 — Cover (cream)
- **Eyebrow:** MARKETING PLAN · OCTOBER 2026
- **Title:** Bringing new people to Rang De
- **Subtitle:** A campaign built on stories, trust and word of mouth, where every touchpoint leads back to the website.
- **Image:** showreel.jpg

### Slide 2 — The challenge
- **Eyebrow:** THE CHALLENGE
- **Title:** Most people will never search for "social investing"
- **Three cards:**
  1. **A new category:** Social investing has to be explained before anyone can want it.
  2. **Advice defaults elsewhere:** Ask friends where to invest and you hear mutual funds and stocks, not Rang De.
  3. **Trust travels person to person:** What people say about Rang De matters more than what the website says.
- **Line:** So the job of marketing is to start the conversation, and let the website finish it.

### Slide 3 — Who we're talking to
- **Eyebrow:** AUDIENCES
- **Title:** Two kinds of first-time investors
- **Card 1 · Mission-driven:**
  - Starts small and wants to see who they helped.
  - Moved by stories.
  - Hook: a real person and a clear need.
- **Card 2 · Corporate professionals:**
  - Earn well and want their money to do social good, with returns.
  - Moved by proof.
  - Hook: RBI regulation, track record, repayments.
- **Line:** Same stories, different proof. Lead with the person; back it with the numbers.

### Slide 4 — What already works (data)
- **Eyebrow:** RANG DE'S OWN INSTAGRAM · 27 REELS
- **Title:** Two reels went past 150,000 views. Both opened the same way.

| Reel | Views | Opening |
|---|---|---|
| "You've heard of investing in stocks. But what about investing in people?" | 150k | Reframes something familiar, then names real people |
| A reel introducing Gayatri, Kukhol and Mamta | 176k | Opens on the viewer's own world, then three real names |
| Naina's story (Project Lehar) | 40.6k | One personal arc |
| Statements and event promos | 1k–9k | A line about Rang De, not a person |

- **Line:** Start from the viewer's world, then meet real, named people. Statements about the brand stay small.

### Slide 5 — Four rules for every asset (dark)
- **Title:** How we talk
- **A journey, not a tutorial:** Follow a person, not a product walkthrough.
- **Stories over stats:** Real entrepreneurs first; every figure sourced.
- **No ₹ in headlines:** Amounts live in the small print, not the hook.
- **No guilt:** Respect, not comparison. Get to know them.

### Slide 6 — The funnel
- **Eyebrow:** HOW IT CONNECTS
- **Title:** Every asset points to one place: a real person on rangde.in
- **Four steps across:**
  1. **Reach:** Investors forward cards · WhatsApp Status · Rang De Channel · Instagram Reels · "Message us" ads
  2. **Chat:** They message us → auto-reply → one real entrepreneur, or a 3-tap matcher
  3. **Website:** A link straight to a person, or to the live opportunities
  4. **Sign up:** eKYC → first investment
- **Line:** WhatsApp only allows messaging people who opted in, so reach comes from people who already trust Rang De, and from people who message us first.

### Slide 10 — Four-week plan (table)
- **Title:** Four weeks, one story at a time

| Week | Investors forward | Channel & Status | Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entrepreneur of the week + ask to share | Story post · showreel | Small "Message us" test |
| 2 | Fund her by Friday | Friday Status card · quiz post | Keep the best ad, test the showreel |
| 3 | Friends pool + next story | Pool card · swipe game post | Pool-focused creative |
| 4 | Fund her by Friday (next person) | Ripple game post · story post | Scale what worked |

- **Line:** At most one or two marketing messages a week per person, always with a way to opt out.

### Slide 11 — How we'll measure it
- **Title:** One number that matters: cost per first investment
- **Path:** Forwards and clicks → visits from WhatsApp → sign-ups → first investment → cost per first investment
- **Line:** Every link is tagged (utm_source=whatsapp, plus medium and campaign), so each card, game and post shows up separately. A personal code per investor shows who brought whom.

### Slide 12 — Guardrails
- **Title:** Trust is the product, so the marketing has to protect it
- Only message people who opted in; honour "STOP".
- Returns always "up to x% p.a.", never guaranteed; "Investments carry risk".
- "RBI-registered NBFC-P2P"; RBI does not assure repayment of P2P loans.
- Consent from featured entrepreneurs for marketing use of photos and names.
- Every figure re-checked on rangde.in before each send.
- Message templates approved by Meta before use.
