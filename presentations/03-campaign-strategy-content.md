### 5 — WHAT WORKS AT THESE STAGES? (two columns, four rows each, with dividers)

**TOFU · Top of Funnel**
1. Care before category. Nobody is evaluating us yet, so explaining P2P answers a question nobody asked.
2. One real person, specific enough to feel true: a name, a place, a need. We claim nothing; their story does the work.
3. Broad reach, emotion-led, a journey not a tutorial. Stories travel by forward, from people they already trust.
4. What fails: ₹ amounts in the headline, product explainers, guilt or comparison, and any statistic we can't source.

**MOFU · Middle of Funnel**
1. Trust, earned. They've met a story; now they need to see it's real, and that their money is looked after.
2. Show the process rather than claim it: met on the ground, business checked, repayment plan agreed, RBI-registered.
3. Narrower, more rational, proof-shaped: track record, monthly repayments, the founders' weekly Q&A.
4. What fails: repeating the top-of-funnel hook, "Invest now" before risk is answered, and returns we can't guarantee.
