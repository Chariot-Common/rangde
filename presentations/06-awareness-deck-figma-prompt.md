## Design system

**Mood:** editorial and calm, dark and warm. No gradients, photos, icons, emoji or drop shadows. Hierarchy comes from type and a few flat panels.

**Colours**
- Background (every slide): #12110E
- Panel / card fill: #1C1B17
- Divider lines: #2A2823 (1px)
- Heading text: #EFEDE6
- Body text: #A8A49B
- Table header / label text: #CFCBC2
- Accent blue (eyebrows, numbers, highlights): #3EAAE0
- Statement blue (one card per slide at most, and the closing slide background): #2E73B8, with #EFEDE6 text
- Caution amber (only the "Before we start" eyebrow): #E3A04A

**Type**
- Headings: Source Serif 4, SemiBold 600
- Everything else: Inter (Light 300 for body, SemiBold 600 for labels)
- Scale:
  - Cover title 120 (line-height 1.02, letter-spacing −2)
  - Slide title 64 (line-height 1.08)
  - Big number 56
  - Card title 36–44 (line-height 1.1)
  - Table first column 30
  - Body 24–26 (line-height 1.45–1.5)
  - Eyebrow 24, uppercase, letter-spacing 2, accent blue
- Nothing smaller than 24px.

**Layout**
- Padding 96px top/bottom and 128px left/right; content area 1664px wide.
- Every slide starts with an eyebrow (uppercase, blue) and then the slide title, 20px apart, at the same position on every slide.
- 48–56px gap from the title block to the content.
- Cards:
  - fill #1C1B17, radius 16, padding 36–48, 24px gaps;
  - 3 columns are 538px each, 4 columns are 398px each, 2 columns are 820px each;
  - inside a card: title (serif), then body (Inter Light), 14–28px apart.
- Tables are rows separated by 1px #2A2823 lines, 20px vertical padding, columns separated by 40px:
  - header row in label style (24, uppercase, letter-spacing 2, #CFCBC2);
  - first column in serif 30 #EFEDE6;
  - other columns in Inter Light 24 #A8A49B.
- "Callout" = a full-width card holding a 300px uppercase label on the left and 26px text on the right.
