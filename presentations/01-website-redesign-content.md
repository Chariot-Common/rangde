## Part 1 · Why we redesigned

### Slide 1 — Cover
- **Eyebrow:** WEBSITE + MARKETING CASE STUDY · OCTOBER 2026
- **Title:** Rang De, redesigned
- **Subtitle:** What first-time visitors told us, what we changed on the homepage, and the campaign we built from the same findings.
- **Logo:** Rang De logo on a cream tile, top-left
- **Notes:** Frame it as three parts: what we heard from people who had never used Rang De, what we changed because of it, and how the same insight shaped the marketing.

### Slide 2 — The brief
- **Eyebrow:** THE BRIEF
- **Title:** Help someone who has never heard of Rang De understand it, trust it, and act.
- **Three cards:**
  1. **What the site said it sells:** "A peer-to-peer social investment platform enabling financial inclusion."
  2. **What visitors are buying:** A useful place for money that would sit idle, where they can see the human impact it creates.
  3. **Two jobs for the homepage** (dark card): Get a new visitor to invest. Get them to tell a friend.
- **Notes:** Social investing is a category Rang De created, so it needs explaining. The old homepage often spoke as if visitors already understood it.

### Slide 3 — Seven questions
- **Eyebrow:** BEFORE ANYONE INVESTS
- **Title:** A first-time visitor asks seven questions
- **Grid of 8 tiles:**
  - Is this safe?
  - Do I get my money back?
  - Who exactly gets my money?
  - Why borrow from me, not a bank?
  - How much can I earn?
  - What if they don't repay?
  - Why not a mutual fund?
  - **[blue tile]** These are conversion questions.
- **Line below:** The old homepage answered few of them before it asked for money.

## Part 2 · How we listened

### Slide 4 — Method
- **Eyebrow:** HOW WE LISTENED
- **Title:** We watched people meet Rang De for the first time
- **Four numbered cards:**
  - **01 Heuristic review:** Every section of rangde.in, screen by screen, as a first-time visitor.
  - **02 1:1 interviews:** Non-investors: a startup founder, a senior developer, a product audit session, and two OCI investors.
  - **03 One guide:** 30–40 minutes: a cold read, mental model, risk and trust, human connection, friction.
  - **04 Affinity map:** Pain points clustered into themes, counted by how many people hit them.
- **Line below:** We didn't explain Rang De first. Their first read of the homepage told us what landed and what didn't.

### Slide 5 — Six themes (table)

| Theme | In their words, roughly |
|---|---|
| "Social investing" isn't self-explanatory | Is this a donation, a loan or an investment? ₹500 felt like a donation. |
| The homepage can't explain itself | One participant had to browse the whole site before it made sense. |
| Trust has to come before any button | Without proof up front, it "might be fake", so no clicks at all. |
| Too many first steps | Four competing buttons, and no idea what happens after clicking. |
| Human stories need to be louder | Cropped photos felt like stock images; "3–10 lives" was abstract. |
| ₹500 vs ₹10,000 | The big ₹10,000 next to the ₹500 minimum "feels like a lie". |

### Slide 6 — Statement (88px serif)
- **Eyebrow:** THE PATTERN BEHIND EVERY INTERVIEW
- **Title:** The site asked people to trust "social investment" before it had explained it, or earned it.
- **Line:** So the redesign does two things in that order: explain, then earn trust. Only then ask.

## Part 3 · Problem → fix (before/after)

### Slide 7 — 01 · The hero
- **Title:** From "one loan at a time" to what it actually is
- **Before:** old-hero.jpg
- **After:** new-hero.jpg
- **We heard:** "One loan at a time" read as the visitor taking a loan. Lend, Invest Now and Events all competed at the top.
- **We changed:** The headline says it plainly: invest, get repaid with interest. One main action, a "See how it works" walkthrough, and proof right below.

### Slide 8 — 02 · Trust signals
- **Title:** The strongest proof moved from the bottom to the first screen
- **Before:** old-stats.jpg (near the footer)
- **After:** new-hero.jpg (under the headline)
- **We heard:** RBI, repayment and reach were shown one at a time, and the best numbers sat in "Our Progress" at the very end.
- **We changed:** Five proof points in one row: entrepreneurs funded, amount invested, the RBI seal, investors, principal repaid. They count up as the page opens.

### Slide 9 — 03 · Why Rang De
- **Title:** A bento with no reading order became one clear story
- **Before:** old-bento.jpg
- **After:** new-why-current.jpg
- **We heard:** Eyes jumped everywhere, the language was technical ("without credit access"), and the video and RBI tile didn't connect.
- **We changed:** Problem → Solution → Impact in plain words. Each stat is underlined and opens its source, so the claim earns trust on the spot.

### Slide 10 — 04 · Who the money goes to
- **Title:** Cropped faces became real people you can choose
- **Before:** old-investors.jpg
- **After:** new-opps.jpg
- **We heard:** Cropped photos felt like stock, and people couldn't answer "who am I investing in?" until far down the page.
- **We changed:** Live listings, one card per person: name, place, what they need, how far along. Returns and rating open on hover, so the person comes first.

### Slide 11 — 05 · The calculator · partly open (amber eyebrow)
- **Title:** Impact now has faces. The starting amount still needs work.
- **Before:** old-calc.jpg
- **After:** new-calc.jpg
- **We heard:** ₹10,000 shown bigger than the ₹500 minimum "feels like a lie", and "3–10 lives" meant little.
- **We changed:** Real entrepreneurs' faces and a clear amount repaid.
- **Still open:** It starts at ₹1,00,000, which anchors high. It should start near ₹500.

### Slide 12 — 06 · Risk, answered early (text left, screenshot right)
- **Title:** "Up to 8.5%" was loud. "What if they don't repay?" was hidden.
- **Points:**
  - FAQ rebuilt around 13 real questions from rangde.in, risk first: how you get your money back, and that RBI rules mean repayment can't be guaranteed.
  - Carets instead of plus signs, and "See all FAQs" for the rest.
  - A trust line under every opportunity: ₹500 minimum · Secured by Razorpay · Regulated by RBI.
- **Image:** new-faq.jpg

### Slide 13 — 07 · The people behind it
- **Title:** Real investors, and founders you can talk to every week
- **Images:** new-testi.jpg, new-founder.jpg
- **Social proof:** Real investors with photos and countries, in two rows that run edge to edge.
- **Founders:** The old site had no faces. Now Ram and Smita's weekly Q&A is one click away.

### Slide 14 — 08 · The details (table)

| What we changed | Why |
|---|---|
| Faster first load on phones | The site took too long to open on mobile |
| Flags as images, not emoji | On Windows, flags showed as letters: "INUSDEAUGBSE" |
| Bold, never black type; one brand blue for numbers | Calmer reading, and numbers match the slider |
| Brand fonts in every mock-up | A Figma font name had fallen back to a serif |
| Real footage at 1080p, full width | The old loop was pixelated; the break didn't land |
| Rows fill wide and zoomed-out screens | Below 50% zoom, sections sat to the left |

## Part 4 · Credibility

### Slide 15 — Every number had to survive a source check

| Claim on the page | What we found | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Only 11% of rural adults have bank credit | No source found | Removed |
| Just 5% of villages have a bank nearby | 99.92% have an outlet within 5 km (Finance Ministry, Aug 2026) | Removed |
| 73% of farm households borrow informally | NABARD 2021–22 says 23.4% | Replaced |
| Credit starts at 8.5% p.a. | 8.5% is the investor return, not the borrower rate | Reworded |
| Nearly half of rural households borrow informally | NABARD survey, July 2026 | Added, sourced |
| 86% lack formal credit | Rang De's own figure; source not yet shared | Flagged |

## Part 5 · Marketing

### Slide 16 — Part two · Marketing
- **Title:** Bring new people in by making them care, before explaining anything.
- **Four cards:**
  - **A journey, not a tutorial:** Follow a person, not a product walkthrough.
  - **Stories over stats:** Real entrepreneurs first; every figure sourced.
  - **No ₹ in headlines:** Amounts live in the small print, not the hook.
  - **No guilt:** Respect, not comparison. Get to know them.

### Slide 17 — WhatsApp + Rang De Play
- **Image:** story-rekha.png (WhatsApp card)
- **Title:** Small things people forward, that all lead back to the site
- **Points:**
  - **Story cards and Status videos** of real, live entrepreneurs, with ready-to-paste messages and an in-chat matcher.
  - **Jyoti Ben's journey:** help a papad maker find a loan. The bank, a finance company and a moneylender each fail her. Rang De appears only at the end.
  - **Guess the gap:** four questions on rural credit, each answer with its source.
- **Footer:** rangde-play.vercel.app · every link tagged so WhatsApp visits show up in analytics

### Slide 18 — The showreel
- **Title:** A 40-second film: get to know them, then help them grow
- **Images:** two stills from rangde-showreel-16x9.mp4 (the search screen; Durga's card)
- **Style:** Modelled frame by frame on the Razorpay × OpenAI launch film: clean type that blurs into focus, a camera that pushes into the product, glass stats over real footage.
- **Story:** Real Rang De footage, a 3D collage of live entrepreneurs, and Durga's verification step by step. 9:16 for Reels, 16:9 for the web, plus an interactive page.

## Part 6 · Next
