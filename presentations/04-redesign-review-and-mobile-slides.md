### Mobile slide C — "Small fixes that matter on mobile"
Layout: like the "Ownership & Access" slide — 6 text cards in a 3 × 2 grid.

1. **16px gutters everywhere** — Text never touches the screen edge.
2. **Swiping you can see** — Cards peek in from both sides, so people know to swipe.
3. **Nothing pokes out** — Stacked cards share one height on scroll.
4. **The video plays on iPhone** — A lighter 720p file that iPhones autoplay.
5. **Bigger tap targets** — Buttons are at least 48px; the walkthrough steps are easier to hit.
6. **Less empty space** — Tighter gaps between sections, sized for a phone screen.
